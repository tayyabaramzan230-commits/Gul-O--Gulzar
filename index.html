Our Journey

About Gul-o-Gulzar

A creative startup originating from passion for art and handmade craft, blooming into a trusted event brand.

Our Story

Gul-o-Gulzar Events began with school decoration, board displays, and detailed art & craft projects. Driven by creativity, we have grown into a full-scale event decoration and coordination company in Karachi.

Our Vision

To become a trusted household name in creative event decoration across Pakistan, widely recognized for uncompromising quality, originality, and warm, client-first service.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, customized, and memorable decor that reflects each client's unique story, while actively encouraging and supporting local handmade artisans.
